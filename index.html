      Our kitchen story

      About Food Recipe Finder

      Welcome to Food Recipe Finder! This website was created to help food lovers discover delicious recipes, explore different cuisines, and find inspiration in cooking. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced home cook, there's always something new to try.

      Use the search bar to look up a dish by name, or switch to ingredient search and cook with whatever is already in your fridge. Save anything you love to your favorites, and let “Surprise Me!” decide dinner for you.

      Where the recipes come from

      All recipe data and food photography on this site come from TheMealDB, a free and open recipe API. Nothing here is invented or hard-coded.

      When TheMealDB provides a source, each recipe links to the original publisher's page under “View Original Recipe”. Those links belong to their respective publishers — this site does not claim ownership of them.
